What Happens When…

One place for the whole "What Happens When…" library — spoken-ready mechanism walk-throughs across platform, cloud, data, languages, AI and robotics. Every volume keeps the same shape: the mechanism, an architect lens, and the terms to land. Open any volume below; each one is a standalone page you can read, search, filter, and print to A4.

No volumes match that. Clear the search or pick another group.

How to use: open index.html in any browser — no server, nothing to install. Click a card to open that volume; use its "← Index" link (top-left) to come back. Everything works offline; an internet connection only makes the web fonts load.
To print: open a volume and print to PDF or A4 (the controls and nav hide automatically; cards avoid splitting across pages).
To add a future volume: drop its standalone .html into this folder and add one entry to volumes.js — the card appears here automatically.
To share: zip this whole folder and send it — it's fully self-contained and portable.
